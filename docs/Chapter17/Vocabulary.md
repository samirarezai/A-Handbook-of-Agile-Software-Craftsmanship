## Terms

- **Code smell**: A surface sign that often indicates a deeper design problem.
- **Heuristic**: A rule of thumb that guides decisions, not a proof.
- **DRY**: “Don’t Repeat Yourself” — avoid duplication to reduce errors and raise abstraction.
- **Law of Demeter**: A guideline to reduce transitive navigation and keep coupling low (“shy code”).
- **Magic number**: A value whose meaning isn’t self-evident in context and should usually be named.
